Payouts — getting your tUSD8 out

Earned tUSD8 sitting in your wallet? Three ways out. Each one is labelled honestly: what's fully real on testnet, and what is a quoted request whose bank settlement is a production step.

Withdraw as crypto REAL

Send your tUSD8 to any external wallet address on Aevum. This is a real on-chain transfer, settled immediately.

Convert to another token UNAVAILABLE

Swap tUSD8 for another stablecoin or token.

No swap route exists on the Aevum testnet yet: Q-Swap, QDay's DEX, currently runs on QDay Origin mainnet only (per the official QDay Wiki) and is announced to follow on Aevum. Rather than fake a swap, conversion stays off until a real route ships.

Withdraw to local currency QUOTE + REQUEST

Get a transparent quote at the live rate, see the fee before you commit, and record a payout request. Bank settlement itself is a licensed-partner production step — on testnet the request is recorded as a simulation, never shown as a real bank credit.
